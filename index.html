01

Q-Matrix Explorer

A two-player grid soccer game solved exactly as a zero-sum Markov game. Drag the players anywhere on the board, hand either one the ball, and watch the payoff matrix and equilibrium mixed strategies update live. It compares stochastic and deterministic move rules, games with and without a STAND action, and the exact solver against trained Nash-DQN and PPO agents, which can also play full matches against each other.

02

Dog Yard Playground

Two players each pull a dog toward their own house, and every round the dog moves to a weighted blend of where it was and where each player pulled. Choose who controls each side, from trained A2C agents to best-response players, drag the houses around, tune the pull share, the dog's inertia and the maximum step, and trace the paths that lead to equilibrium.
